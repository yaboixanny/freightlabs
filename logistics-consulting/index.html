Quick answers

What you might be wondering.

01What problems can logistics consulting address?

Working togethers can cover go-to-market strategy, sales process, marketing, technology, workflows, measurement, and operational growth constraints.

02Do you implement the recommendations?

Scope can include advisory only or hands-on implementation support with your team and selected vendors.

03Who should participate?

The right group depends on the problem and may include ownership, operations, sales, marketing, finance, and technology leaders.

04How long is an engagement?

Timing ranges from a focused diagnostic to ongoing advisory. The proposal defines outcomes, milestones, and responsibilities.

05Do you recommend specific technology?

When relevant, we evaluate requirements and tradeoffs before recommending tools, integrations, or vendor options.

06How will progress be measured?

Measures are selected around the engagement and can include pipeline, conversion, margin, cycle time, utilization, and implementation milestones.
