What we see all the time

The website does not help sales

Even good visitors can get stuck on a vague website. Freight and 3PL buyers need clear services, useful proof, and an easy next step.

SEO by logistics model

Different operations require different search strategies.

The right plan depends on what you sell, where you operate, and what buyers need to see before they ask for a quote. Here is what we would focus on for four common logistics businesses.

3PL SEO

SEO for 3PL companies

3PL buyers usually search by service, location, industry, and order profile—not simply “3PL.” Build pages around the combinations you can actually serve.

Search opportunities
E-commerce 3PL, retail distribution, omnichannel fulfillment, reverse logistics
High-value pages
Fulfillment, warehousing, transportation management, industries, and real facility locations
Proof buyers need
Facility data, integrations, SLAs, accuracy, customer examples, and onboarding steps
Ask every lead
Order volume, pallets, SKUs, geography, integrations, and start date

Trucking SEO

SEO for trucking companies

Focus on the freight you can move well: equipment, lanes, markets, commodities, and service level. Keep shipper and driver content in separate paths.

Search opportunities
Reefer carrier, dedicated trucking, cross-border FTL, regional flatbed
High-value pages
Equipment, modes, real lanes and markets, industries, and staffed terminals
Proof buyers need
USDOT authority, safety, equipment, coverage, tracking, and on-time performance
Ask every lead
Origin, destination, mode, commodity, frequency, and timing
See our SEO for trucking companies approach →
Forwarding SEO

SEO for freight forwarders

Forwarding searches split by mode, trade lane, urgency, customs needs, and commodity. Each important service needs its own useful page.

Search opportunities
Air freight forwarder, ocean forwarding, customs help, project cargo
High-value pages
Air, ocean, customs, project cargo, and the trade lanes you genuinely serve
Proof buyers need
Gateways, schedules, partner network, documentation, tracking, and handling experience
Ask every lead
Origin, destination, mode, commodity, dimensions, and urgency
See our freight forwarding marketing approach →
Warehousing SEO

SEO for warehousing and fulfillment providers

Warehouse buyers compare location and capability together. A facility page should quickly show whether the building fits their operation.

Search opportunities
Food-grade warehouse, Shopify fulfillment, cross-dock facility, cold storage
High-value pages
Each real facility, services, integrations, industries, and onboarding
Proof buyers need
Square footage, pallet positions, docks, temperature range, certifications, and cutoffs
Ask every lead
Pallets or orders, SKUs, storage needs, integrations, seasonality, and start date

Quick answers

What you might be wondering.

01How long does logistics SEO take?

Technical improvements can appear early, while competitive commercial rankings typically require sustained work over several months.

02Do you write the content?

Yes. Content planning and production can include service pages, location pages, guides, and updates to existing copy.

03Can you support local and national SEO?

Yes. The strategy depends on whether buyers choose a nearby facility, a regional provider, or a national partner.

04What about AI search visibility?

Clear entities, useful expert content, structured information, and strong authority support visibility across traditional and AI-assisted search.

05Do you guarantee rankings?

No responsible SEO partner can guarantee a position. We define controllable deliverables and report movement, qualified traffic, and leads.

06How is performance reported?

Reporting can include indexation, priority rankings, organic landing pages, qualified inquiries, and pipeline signals.
