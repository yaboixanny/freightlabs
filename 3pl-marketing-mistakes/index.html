5 Common Mistakes in 3PL Marketing

Book a Strategy Call

3PLs face some tough marketing challenges. Buyers may struggle to tell providers apart. They may also be wary of switching. This guide covers five common mistakes and how to fix them.

This guide will help you refine your 3PL SEO strategy to avoid these pitfalls and drive real growth.

Mistake #1: Leading with Features Instead of Outcomes

Many 3PL sites list services like warehousing, fulfillment, and freight management. That tells buyers what you do, but not how you help their business.

Shippers care about orders arriving on time, keeping costs down, and handling busy seasons. Your marketing should show how you solve those problems.

How to Fix It

  • Reframe your services in terms of customer benefits: "Reduce fulfillment costs by 20%" rather than "We provide fulfillment services"
  • Use customer language gathered from sales conversations and interviews
  • Lead with the problem you solve, then explain how your services address it
  • Include specific metrics and results wherever possible

Mistake #2: Targeting Everyone Instead of Someone

Saying “we serve every industry” can make it hard for buyers to see what you do best. In a crowded market, a broad message can also leave you competing mostly on price.

Some 3PLs focus on fields such as food, cosmetics, medical devices, or e-commerce. Knowing a field well can help you stand out, support your pricing, and earn referrals.

How to Fix It

  • Analyze your current customer base to find where you've had the most success
  • Develop industry-specific content, case studies, and landing pages
  • Pursue relevant certifications that show specialized capability
  • Attend and sponsor industry-specific trade shows rather than general logistics events

Mistake #3: Neglecting the Website as a Sales Tool

Many 3PL sites list services but do little to guide buyers. Your site should help the right people see if you fit their needs and make it easy to get in touch.

Common website shortcomings include unclear value propositions, missing contact details, no compelling calls to action, and content that hasn't been updated in years. These issues signal to potential customers that your company may not be keeping up with the times. A strong logistics website gives buyers a clearer path to assess fit and get in touch.

How to Fix It

  • Clarify your value proposition on the homepage—visitors should understand what you do and for whom within seconds
  • Add clear calls to action on every page: "Get a Quote," "Schedule a Consultation," "Download Our Guide"
  • Create lead magnets like ROI calculators, industry guides, or checklists to capture contact details
  • Implement live chat or chatbots to engage visitors in real-time

Mistake #4: Inconsistent Marketing Execution

Marketing takes steady work. Some 3PLs run a campaign when business is slow, then stop when things get busy. Starting and stopping can waste the work already done.

Good marketing can build value over time. A useful blog post may bring visitors for years. Staying in touch with industry publications can also help more people learn about your business.

How to Fix It

  • Develop a marketing calendar with planned activities for the entire year
  • Allocate dedicated resources—either internal staff or an agency partner—to ensure execution
  • Focus on sustainable activities rather than one-time campaigns
  • Track metrics monthly to maintain accountability and show progress

Mistake #5: Ignoring Customer Stories and Social Proof

Trust matters when changing providers is costly. Yet many 3PLs have no case studies, customer reviews, or testimonials on their sites.

Buyers want to know you have helped companies like theirs. They want clear results and feedback from real customers. Without proof, they may be reluctant to choose you.

How to Fix It

  • Systematically collect testimonials from satisfied customers
  • Develop detailed case studies that showcase specific challenges, solutions, and results
  • Request reviews on Google, industry directories, and relevant platforms
  • Feature customer logos prominently on your website and marketing materials
  • Create video testimonials for even more compelling social proof

Moving Forward

Start by checking your marketing against this list. Pick the biggest gaps and make a plan to fix them. Solving these problems can help your 3PL stand out.
