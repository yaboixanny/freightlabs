Quick answers

What you might be wondering.

01Do you understand cold-chain compliance?

We consider FDA requirements, GDP, tracking, monitoring, and handling in your marketing. Your compliance team must approve any regulated claims.

02How do you establish trust without overclaiming?

We use approved facts about your sites, equipment, monitoring, and certifications. We avoid claims you cannot support.

03Can you target pharmaceutical and food buyers separately?

Yes. Their risks, terminology, proof requirements, stakeholders, and buying journeys differ, so campaigns and pages should be separated.

04Which marketing channels should we use?

The right mix depends on your buyers, budget, sales team, and goals. We also look at how quickly you need results.

05How do you measure lead quality?

We work with your sales team to define a good lead. Where your tools allow, we track leads through meetings, quotes, and sales.

06How quickly can we launch?

The timeline depends on the research, ads, tracking, and pages you need. Your proposal will explain each step and what we need to get started.
