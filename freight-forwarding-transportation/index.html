Where inquiries get stuck

Why Freight Forwarders Struggle to Get Quote Requests

A strong forwarding network is only part of the decision. Buyers also need to know which lanes, modes, services, and cargo your team can support before they get in touch.

01

Decisions take time

Choosing a freight forwarder can involve several teams. Buyers may compare networks, rates, and customs support before making a change. Clear information and steady follow-up can help keep your business in mind.

02

Vague messages and referrals

Referrals can bring great customers, but they are hard to predict. If your site only promises “global logistics solutions,” buyers cannot tell if you are a good fit. Show the trade lanes, modes, and freight you handle.

03

Services are hard to compare

Freight rates depend on the route, cargo, timing, and services needed. Without a quote, buyers may struggle to compare providers and focus only on price. Explain what affects cost and what your service includes.

04

Quote requests go cold

A freight quote can take time, especially when a shipment needs input from several partners. Without an update, the shipper may lose interest. Confirm the request, explain what happens next, and keep the buyer informed.
