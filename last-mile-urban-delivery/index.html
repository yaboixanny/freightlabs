Where inquiries get stuck

Why Delivery Companies Struggle to Get Good-Fit Inquiries

Businesses choosing a delivery partner need to know where you operate and what you can handle. Clear service details can help them see whether your network fits their needs.

01

Coverage is hard to pin down

A broad service area does not tell a buyer which cities or delivery types you cover. Show the markets and delivery options your team actually supports.

02

Customer and driver traffic mix

Job seekers and business buyers often arrive at the same website. Give each group a clear path so a shipper can request service without sorting through job listings.

03

Buyers compare more than speed

Tracking, missed deliveries, support, and handling needs all affect the choice. Explain how your service works and use claims your team can back up.

04

Requests lack delivery details

Without locations, volume, package needs, and timing, a quote can take longer to prepare. Ask for useful details and keep the buyer informed while your team reviews them.
