Quick answers

AI SEO questions, answered.

01What is AI SEO?

AI SEO helps search engines and AI answer tools understand, retrieve, trust, and cite your company’s information. You may also hear it called generative engine optimization, GEO, answer engine optimization, AEO, or LLM optimization.

02Is AI SEO different from traditional SEO?

They overlap. Technical SEO, crawlable pages, useful content, links, and authority still matter. AI SEO puts extra focus on entity clarity, answer formatting, source credibility, and visibility across answer platforms.

03Can you guarantee ChatGPT citations or AI Overviews?

No. No agency controls those answers. We improve the technical, content, and authority signals that make your company easier to understand and more useful to cite.

04How long does AI SEO take?

Some fixes can go live quickly, but durable visibility normally develops over several months as pages are crawled, trusted, referenced, and improved.

05Which AI platforms do you optimize for?

We focus on the shared web signals used across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, and other answer experiences rather than chasing one platform-specific trick.

06How do you measure AI search visibility?

We monitor a fixed set of relevant prompts, brand mentions, cited sources, AI referral traffic, organic search trends, and assisted leads. Prompt results vary, so no single visibility score tells the whole story.
