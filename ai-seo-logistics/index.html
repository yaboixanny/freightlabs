Your AI Search Questions, Answered

01What is AI SEO?

AI SEO helps make your company’s services and expertise easier to find and understand in AI-powered search. It builds on a strong website and useful content.

02What is an AEO agency for logistics companies?

An answer engine optimization (AEO) agency helps logistics companies create clear, useful content for questions buyers ask. Freight Labs focuses on freight, warehousing, 3PL, and other logistics businesses. We work to make your services easier for people and AI tools to understand, without promising a mention or citation.

03What’s the difference between GEO/AEO and traditional SEO?

Traditional SEO helps your pages appear in search results. Generative engine optimization (GEO) and answer engine optimization (AEO) focus on making your information useful in AI-generated answers. They overlap: a well-built site and helpful content support both.

04Do AI SEO services replace my current SEO?

No. AI SEO builds on your existing SEO. We can improve your site’s technical health, service pages, company details, and content while continuing to work on organic search visibility.

05Can you guarantee ChatGPT citations or AI Overviews?

No. No agency controls those answers. We improve the technical, content, and trust signals that make your company easier to understand and cite.

06How fast will AI platforms start recommending our brand?

There is no fixed timeline or guarantee. Updates can be published quickly, but AI visibility varies by platform, competition, and how often sources are updated. We track relevant questions and mentions over time to see what changes.

07Why does specialization in logistics matter for AEO?

Logistics buyers ask specific questions about services, lanes, equipment, capacity, and compliance. Industry knowledge helps us explain those details clearly and create content that is useful to the shippers and partners you want to reach.

08What content formats work best for AEO in logistics?

Start with the format that best answers the buyer’s question. Clear service pages, comparisons, FAQs, and how-to guides can explain your capabilities. Include accurate details about your real services and operations, and keep them up to date.

09Which AI platforms do you optimize for?

We focus on the web signals shared by Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, and other AI tools.

10How do you measure AI search visibility?

We track key prompts, brand mentions, cited sources, AI visits, search trends, and leads. No single score tells the whole story.
