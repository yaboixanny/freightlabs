Quick answers

AI SEO questions, answered.

01What is AI SEO?

AI SEO helps search engines and AI tools understand your company, trust its information, and use it in their answers.

02Is AI SEO different from traditional SEO?

They overlap. Technical SEO and helpful content still matter. AI SEO also makes your services, answers, and trusted sources easier for AI tools to understand.

03Can you guarantee ChatGPT citations or AI Overviews?

No. No agency controls those answers. We improve the technical, content, and trust signals that make your company easier to understand and cite.

04How long does AI SEO take?

Some fixes can go live quickly. Lasting visibility often takes several months as search engines crawl, trust, and improve your pages.

05Which AI platforms do you optimize for?

We focus on the web signals shared by Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, and other AI tools.

06How do you measure AI search visibility?

We track key prompts, brand mentions, cited sources, AI visits, search trends, and leads. No single score tells the whole story.
