Alex’s story

From the driver’s seat to Freight Labs.

Freight Labs started with a real connection to transportation, an unexpected career change, and a pretty obvious gap in the logistics market.

01 / On the road

It started with flatbed.

Alex started out driving flatbed in Canada. Long days, tight schedules, jobsite delays, and constant pressure taught him how transportation businesses actually work—not how they look in a marketing deck.

02 / The pivot

An accident changed the plan.

After a motorcycle accident left him injured, Alex had to step away from driving and figure out what came next. That detour led him into digital marketing, where he learned how strong positioning, search, paid media, and better websites turn attention into real sales.

03 / The gap

Logistics deserved better marketing.

After working across a wide range of industries and niches, he looked back at logistics and saw a clear opportunity. Many great legacy businesses still relied heavily on referrals, dated websites, and inconsistent marketing. Very few agencies offered modern, high-quality work built around how freight companies actually sell.

04 / Today

That became Freight Labs.

Today, Alex runs Freight Labs to help 3PLs, freight brokers, carriers, warehouses, and transportation companies get found, bring in better leads, and build a more dependable path to growth.
