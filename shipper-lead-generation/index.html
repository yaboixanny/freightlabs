Your Shipper Lead Generation Questions, Answered

01How is this different from buying a shipper list?

A bought list groups firms by broad traits. An account map starts with your real freight needs. It finds sites, maps buyers, and records why each account may fit.

02Can you target specific lanes?

Yes. We can start with key origins, destinations, regions, modes, equipment, and goods. Then we find accounts that fit that network.

03Who counts as a transportation buyer?

It depends on the firm. It may include transportation and logistics leads, buyers, plant or warehouse teams, supply-chain leaders, and finance leads.

04What makes an account sales-ready?

It needs a real freight fit and enough detail to move on: the freight, a need or timing sign, the right person, and a clear next step.

05Does this replace broad lead generation?

No. This is the focused account option for brokerages that know the freight they want. Our freight broker lead generation service can support a wider outbound plan.
