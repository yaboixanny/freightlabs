What is Freight Labs?

Freight Labs is a marketing agency focused on logistics and transportation businesses. We help companies reach the right buyers and turn more interest into sales conversations.

What services does Freight Labs offer?

Who founded Freight Labs?

Alex founded Freight Labs after working in transportation and marketing. His experience driving flatbed in Canada helps shape how we understand logistics businesses.

How is a logistics marketing agency different from a general agency?

A logistics-focused agency builds its work around freight services, shipper needs, and long sales cycles. That context helps make your marketing clearer to the buyers you want to reach.
