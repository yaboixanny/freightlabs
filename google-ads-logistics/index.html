Your Google Ads Questions, Answered

01How fast will I see leads?

With Google Ads, we often see the first leads come in within one or two weeks. They may not all be the right fit, but they give us an early look at who the campaigns are reaching. If leads aren’t coming in, we look at the campaigns, landing pages, budget, and other factors. The first month is about building a baseline. From there, we use what we learn to improve lead quality and work toward consistent results before scaling.

02Do you understand DOT and FMCSA compliance?

We use your business details to shape ads and targeting. Your team must approve all regulatory statements, authority details, and claims before they go live.

03What is your minimum ad spend?

There isn’t a universal minimum, but for most clients we recommend starting with at least $100 per day in ad spend. We’ll review your services, markets, and estimated keyword costs in Google Ads Keyword Planner, then share a budget recommendation based on what we find. With a smaller budget, it can take longer to collect enough data to improve lead quality. If you’re working with less than about $100 per day, your budget may go further with cold email, LinkedIn outreach, or Facebook ads, depending on your audience and goals.

04Do you build landing pages?

Yes. We can build or improve landing pages for your services. Each page should match the search, explain your offer, and help screen leads. Learn more about our logistics web design services.

05Can you track calls and sales outcomes?

Yes. Attribution is a big part of how we work at Freight Labs. We track calls and quote requests, qualify the leads coming in, and work with your team to get feedback on lead quality. When your CRM and tracking setup support it, we can feed qualified lead data back into Google Ads. That gives Google better information to optimize your campaigns toward the leads that matter most to your business over time.

06Will you run Performance Max or Local Services Ads?

Only when they fit. Search is often the control layer; Performance Max needs suitable data and creative, while Local Services Ads depend on Google’s category and location eligibility.
