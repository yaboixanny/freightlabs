Where inquiries get stuck

Why Supply Chain Technology Companies Struggle to Get Demo Requests

A strong product can be hard to explain in a crowded market. Buyers need to see how it fits their operation before they commit time to a demo.

01

Features without context

A long feature list does not tell an operations team what will improve. Connect each capability to a real problem, workflow, and buyer need.

02

Different buyers need different proof

Operations, IT, finance, and leadership all bring different questions. Give each group the details it needs to assess fit and share the case internally.

03

Integration questions slow decisions

Buyers want to know how a new tool works with the systems they already use. Explain supported connections, setup, and the role your team plays.

04

Demo requests lose momentum

A demo request can go cold if follow-up takes too long or lacks context. Record the buyer’s needs, assign an owner, and agree on a clear next step.
