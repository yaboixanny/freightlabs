The commercial challenge

Technical capability is not the same as a clear buying case.

Logistics technology buyers evaluate operational fit, integrations, implementation risk, adoption, and return on investment. Generic SaaS messaging misses the realities behind those decisions.

01

Feature-heavy messaging

Long capability lists obscure the operational problem the product actually solves.

02

Multiple stakeholders

Operations, IT, finance, procurement, and executives need different proof before a deal advances.

03

Integration anxiety

Buyers worry about ERP, carrier, marketplace, EDI, data, and implementation complexity.

04

Long demo-to-close cycles

Weak nurturing and sales enablement allow qualified interest to stall after the first demo.

Timing depends on research, tracking, creative, approvals, and landing-page needs. The proposal defines a practical launch sequence and the dependencies for each stage.
