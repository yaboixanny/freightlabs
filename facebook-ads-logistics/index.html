Your Facebook Ads Questions, Answered

01Can Facebook Ads generate B2B logistics leads?

Yes, when the offer, audience, creative, and qualification path are built for a specific commercial buyer. We optimize around useful sales conversations, not maximum form volume.

02How do you target logistics buyers?

We use customer data, CRM lists, job and industry details, and location to reach the right people. We may also use lookalike audiences when enough reliable data is available.

03What is your minimum ad spend?

There is no set minimum. We suggest a range after reviewing your audience, target area, offer, and ad needs.

04Do you create the ads?

Yes. The work can include freight-focused ad copy, images, short video guidance, offers, and a testing plan.

05Do you use native lead forms or landing pages?

Either can work. Native forms reduce friction, while landing pages provide more context and qualification. We select and test the route that best fits the offer and sales process.

06Do you set up Meta Pixel and Conversions API?

Yes, where your website and systems support them. We can also connect forms, calls, CRM stages, and offline events so optimization is informed by lead quality.
