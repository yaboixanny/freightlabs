Why your transportation SEO may not be working.

More traffic does not always mean more freight.
Transportation searches split by mode, market, equipment, commodity, and urgency. The plan has to attract shippers without mixing them up with drivers or low-fit traffic.

Shipper and driver traffic mix

Driver job searches can dominate your traffic. That can make search results look strong even when they bring few shipper leads.

Service areas stay vague

A claim like “nationwide coverage” does not tell buyers where your terminals and lanes are. It also leaves out capacity and border experience.

Mode pages say too little

FTL, LTL, flatbed, reefer, dedicated, and expedited buyers each need a clear page with relevant detail.

×

Trust signals are buried

Buyers need to see your authority, safety record, insurance, equipment, and tracking early. Do not make them search for proof of on-time service.

Your Transportation SEO Questions, Answered

01What does a transportation SEO company do?

A transportation SEO company helps carriers appear in relevant searches. It improves technical site health, builds useful service and market pages, and tracks the inquiries those pages bring.

02How long does transportation SEO take?

Some site fixes can help early. Ranking for competitive services and markets usually takes several months.

03Transportation SEO vs. trucking SEO: which do I need?

Transportation SEO can cover carriers across modes and markets. Trucking SEO focuses on a fleet’s equipment, lanes, services, and the shippers it wants to reach. If you run a trucking company, explore our SEO for trucking companies. Both approaches can overlap.

04Can you target specific lanes and markets?

Yes, when the pages reflect real operations. We build around genuine service areas, terminals, equipment, modes, and industries rather than thin location pages.

05Can you separate shipper leads from driver traffic?

Yes. We create separate paths for shipper services and recruiting so each audience reaches the right content and call to action.

06Do you understand carrier compliance?

We cover the details buyers check. These include USDOT authority, safety, equipment, insurance, tracking, and service areas.
